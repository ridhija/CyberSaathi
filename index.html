LEARN TO SPOT THE TRAP

Phishing Awareness

Phishing is a fake message designed to trick you into clicking a link, paying money, or sharing private information.

🚨

Urgent pressure

“Act now or your account will be blocked.” Scammers try to stop you from thinking carefully.

🔗

Unknown links

Check the sender and website address. Open the official app or type the official address yourself.

🔐

Secrets requested

Legitimate support should not ask you to share your password or OTP in a message.

FICTIONAL TRAINING EXAMPLE

Parcel delivery notice

“Your parcel is on hold. Pay ₹10 immediately using this unfamiliar link or your parcel will be returned.”

Warning signs: unexpected payment, pressure to act quickly, and an unfamiliar link.

What should you do?

1️⃣
Pause

Do not rush or click the link.

2️⃣
Verify

Check through the organisation’s official app or phone number.

3️⃣
Report

Report the message as spam or phishing and delete it.
